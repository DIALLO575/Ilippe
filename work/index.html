01 / FIRST INITIATIVE

Aplunio

IN DEVELOPMENT

Business applications designed to adapt.

Aplunio is being developed as a platform for creating adaptable business applications. Its purpose is to help shape software around the actual workflows and needs of organizations, rather than force every business into a rigid template.

The initiative is still in development. Its own product experience and public information will be presented separately as they become available.
