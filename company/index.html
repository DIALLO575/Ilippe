Illipe is being built as a home for technology initiatives — with a shared direction and the freedom for each one to respond to real needs.

OUR IDENTITYEARLY-STAGE / FOUNDER-LED
01 / Who we are

Built around
what matters.

Illipe is an independent, founder-led technology venture. We seek to understand practical challenges and turn promising ideas into useful solutions.

We are building the foundations for a parent organization capable of developing and supporting distinct technology initiatives over time.

Our first initiative is Aplunio. Rather than presenting a portfolio we haven't built, we prefer to show the work taking shape and the direction behind it.

02 / How we think

An approach rooted in reality.

These are the principles guiding the organization we aim to build. They are commitments to work toward, not claims of having already perfected the process.

01 / CLARITY

Understand first.

Start from people, contexts and problems instead of assuming technology is the answer.

02 / RESPONSIBILITY

Build thoughtfully.

Take product decisions, engineering quality and real-world consequences seriously.

03 / CONTINUITY

Learn and evolve.

Use evidence, feedback and iteration to develop work that can improve over time.

We see Illipe as a foundation for creating and developing meaningful technology — not as a single app, product or trend.
